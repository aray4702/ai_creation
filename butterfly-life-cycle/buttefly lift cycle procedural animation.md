# Butterfly Life Cycle Animation

A smooth, continuous 3D procedural animation in Three.js/WebGL showing the complete metamorphosis of a butterfly.

## Stages
1. **Egg and hatching:** an egg on a leaf hatches and a small caterpillar crawls out.
2. **Larva:** the caterpillar crawls along the stems, eating leaves and growing.
3. **Chrysalis:** it climbs a branch, hangs, and forms a protective chrysalis.
4. **Emergence and flight:** the chrysalis splits, the butterfly emerges with folded wings, spreads them, and flies away on smooth paths.

## Controls
- Play, pause, speed control and camera angles.
