# Interactive 3D Black Hole

Build a detailed, physically inspired 3D black hole viewer in the browser with Three.js/WebGL.

## Visual effects to show
- Light from background stars and dust bent around the black hole (gravitational lensing).
- A bright accretion disk whose particles orbit, coloured by temperature (hotter near the centre).
- Jets of material shooting out along both poles.
- Relativistic lighting: the approaching side of the disk brightened (Doppler beaming) and light reddened as it climbs out of the gravity well.

## User controls
- Three ways to view: fly freely, a slow automatic cinematic orbit, and a steadier "observatory" mode for close study.
- Sliders for disk mass, spin (angular momentum), tilt, and how strongly light is bent.

## One-click presets
- Sagittarius A* (the Milky Way's central black hole)
- M87*
- A typical stellar-mass black hole
- A fast-spinning Kerr black hole
