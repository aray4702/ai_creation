# Interactive Procedural Wallpaper

A lightweight animated wallpaper in a single HTML5 Canvas file, with flowing ambient colour and an interactive particle field.

## Visuals
- Smooth, looping waves of colour driven by simplex/Perlin noise.
- A field of softly glowing particles drifting across the screen.

## Interaction
- Moving the mouse sends ripples through the colour flow and pushes the particles aside.
- Clicking cycles through palette presets such as Deep Space Cyberpunk, Golden Hour Aurora and Oceanic Abyss.

## Performance
- No external dependencies, low CPU/GPU use, and resolution that scales automatically to the screen.
