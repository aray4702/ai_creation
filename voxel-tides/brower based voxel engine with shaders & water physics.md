# Browser Voxel Engine with Shaders and Water

A Minecraft-style voxel sandbox engine running in a single self-contained WebGL HTML file.

## World
- **Terrain:** chunk-based, effectively endless 3D terrain generated from simplex/Perlin noise, with biomes, trees, caves and several block types to choose from.
- **Sky and light:** a continuous day–night cycle with dynamic shadows and sunrise and sunset colours.
- **Water:** moving water with waves, plus reflection and refraction shaders.

## Controls
- First-person pointer-lock movement, breaking and placing blocks, and collision with the world.
