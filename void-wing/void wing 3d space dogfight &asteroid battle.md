# Void Wing: 3D Space Dogfight and Asteroid Battle

A 3D space-combat game: dogfight enemy starships and fly through asteroid fields.

## Assets
- The player's ship, enemy ships, asteroids with detailed surfaces, lasers and explosions are all generated in code, with no external media.

## Flying and fighting
- Pitch, roll, yaw and throttle for full 3D movement.
- Energy blasters with travelling projectiles, hit detection, and heat/ammo management.

## Enemies and hazards
- Enemy ships that manoeuvre, track the player and fire plasma bolts.
- Dense asteroid fields and debris that crack apart or explode when shot.

## Effects and HUD
- Detailed ship lighting, engine thruster particle trails and particle explosions.
- A cockpit/tactical HUD with a crosshair, shield and hull bars, radar, and mission status.

## Sound
- A synthesized deep-space score, laser shots and heavy, bass-rich explosions.
