# Syrup on a Moving Conveyor Belt

A single HTML5/WebGL physics page showing a thin stream of syrup falling straight down onto a horizontal belt that is moving underneath it.

## Behaviour to simulate
- The stream leaves a nozzle and lands on the moving belt.
- Depending on belt speed, drop height and flow rate, the deposit should change between regimes: coiling, meandering, buckling, and a straight line.

## Appearance
- Golden syrup that looks glossy and partly see-through, with sharp specular highlights and reflections.
- A belt surface that visibly scrolls, plus an indicator showing its current speed.

## Controls
- **Belt speed:** from stopped (0 m/s) up to fast.
- **Drop height:** distance from the nozzle down to the belt.
- **Nozzle size and exit speed:** these change how thick the stream is.
- **Live chart:** a real-time plot marking where the pattern changes between coils, figure-eights, zigzags and a straight line.
