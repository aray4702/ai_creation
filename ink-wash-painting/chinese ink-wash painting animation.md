# Chinese Ink-Wash Painting Animation

A procedural 2D/3D canvas animation in the style of traditional Chinese ink-wash painting (水墨画), on textured rice paper.

## Paper and ink
- Black and grey ink that bleeds and spreads across the rice paper realistically.

## Scenes
1. **Mountains and sun:** layered ink-wash mountain ridges, a rising red sun, birds in flight, and a lone boat on the river.
2. **Lotus pond:** ink blooming into broad lotus leaves, with delicate pink lotus flowers on rippling water.
3. **Plum blossoms:** brush strokes sketching a twisted trunk and branches of red plum blossom (梅花).

## Feel
- Smooth, flowing transitions between scenes that imitate how ink and colour soak into paper and blend.
