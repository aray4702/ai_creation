# Procedural Pixel-Art Wizard

A standalone HTML/Canvas page showing an animated retro pixel-art wizard. The sprite is drawn entirely by code, with no image files.

## Colours
- Use a fixed retro palette of exactly 24 colours.

## Three animation states
1. **Idle:** the wizard breathes gently, the tunic sways and the staff hovers.
2. **Charging:** magic gathers around them, with glowing particles swirling and the robe lifting as if in a draft.
3. **Casting:** a burst of energy fires as a particle beam, with screen shake and flashes of light, then the particles fade away.

## Rendering
- Sharp, grid-aligned pixel scaling with no blurring.
- An optional CRT scanline effect.
