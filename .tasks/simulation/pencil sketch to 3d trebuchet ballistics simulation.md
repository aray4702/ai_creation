# Pencil Sketch to 3D Trebuchet Ballistics Simulation

Convert a 2D hand-drawn pencil sketch of a trebuchet into a functional, interactive 3D physics simulation in a single HTML web application.

## Simulation & Ballistics Engine
- **3D Model Generation**: Construct a 3D wooden trebuchet rig matching the structural lines of the original sketch (base, counterweight arm, pivot axle, sling).
- **Physics Calculation**:
  - Rigid body physics modeling rotational inertia, gravitational torque from counterweight, and sling release mechanics.
- **Destructible Target Environment**:
  - Procedural masonry tower target composed of individual stacked physical blocks that crumble realistically upon impact.
- **Interactive Tuning**: Sliders to adjust counterweight mass, payload mass, sling angle, and launch velocity trajectory visualization.
