Build a smooth, continuous 3D procedural animation using Three.js / WebGL that visually demonstrates the complete metamorphosis life cycle of a butterfly.

### Animation Stages:
1. **Stage 1 (Egg & Hatching):** Show an egg attached to a leaf hatching as a small caterpillar emerges.
2. **Stage 2 (Larva Feeding):** Caterpillar crawling along plant stems and eating leaves while growing in size.
3. **Stage 3 (Chrysalis / Pupa):** Caterpillar climbing a branch, suspending itself, and forming a protective pupa/cocoon.
4. **Stage 4 (Emergence & Spread):** Chrysalis breaking open, the butterfly emerging with folded wings, expanding its wings, and flying off in smooth flight paths.
Include playback controls (Play, Pause, Speed Control, Camera Angles).
