Build a 3D third-person historical action combat game set on an ancient Chinese street during the Southern Song Dynasty (南宋).

### Design & Mechanics:
1. **Environment & Visual Style:**
   - Authentic Southern Song architecture: weathered green-brick walls, exposed plaster, traditional tiled roofs, and historic street decorations.
2. **Combat & Martial Arts:**
   - Third-person player character equipped with melee combat abilities against black-clad martial artist foes.
   - Execute distinct attack combos, dodges, and stance transitions.
   - **Weapon Switching:** Toggle between single long blade and dual swords (双刀), each with unique attack animations and hitboxes.
3. **AI & World Interactivity:**
   - Aggressive enemy AI that spawns in groups and engages in tactical melee combat.
   - Environmental collision with realistic spatial layout across historic alleys.
