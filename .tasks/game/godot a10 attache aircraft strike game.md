Build a 3D air combat simulator in the Godot Game Engine where the player pilots an A-10 Warthog attack aircraft on a ground-strike mission.

### Game Mechanics:
1. **Flight Controls:**
   - Smooth 3D flight physics handling pitch, roll, yaw, throttle speed, and evasive maneuvering.
2. **Offensive Ordnance:**
   - Fire high-rate-of-fire cannon rounds and lock-on air-to-ground missiles targeting enemy armor and military installations below.
3. **Enemy Ground Defenses:**
   - Active surface-to-air missile (SAM) batteries on the terrain firing incoming heat-seeking missiles at the player.
   - Implement flare countermeasures and evasive maneuvers to dodge incoming air defense fire.
4. **Impact & Audio Visual Effects:**
   - Ground target explosion particle effects, damage feedback HUD, hit notifications, and plane health tracking.
