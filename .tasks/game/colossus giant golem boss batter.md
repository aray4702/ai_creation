# Colossus: 3D Giant Golem Boss Battle

Develop a third-person 3D action boss fight game where the player battles a massive stone/iron golem boss.

## Requirements & Mechanics
- **Asset Creation Constraint**: Procedurally generate the 3D player character, the colossal golem boss model, arena terrain, weather shaders, and attack effects in code/Blender with zero downloaded external files.
- **Cinematic Boss Assembly Sequence**:
  - Opening loading/intro sequence where the colossal golem's stone/iron body parts physically assemble piece-by-piece in mid-air before crashing onto the arena ground with lighting effects.
- **Boss AI & Terrain Destruction**:
  - Golem boss with high-damage melee slam, sweep, and area-of-effect attacks.
  - **Dynamic Environment Destruction**: Heavy ground impacts from the boss fracture and shatter the arena ground tiles in real time.
- **Player Combat Mechanics**:
  - Melee attack combos, dodge roll with invulnerability windows, stamina consumption, and lock-on camera controls.
  - Health/boss health bars, defeat/respawn loop, rain/storm particle weather systems, and sound effects.

**Execution note:** Blender is not installed: generate every asset procedurally in code.
