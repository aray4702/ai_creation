# 250 Years of American History Sand Animation & Procedural Score

Create a standalone HTML application that renders a 2-minute procedural sand art animation depicting 250 years of American history alongside a self-composed audio soundtrack.

## Animation & Audio Specifications
- **Sand Simulation**:
  - High-density particle system simulating thousands of individual sand grains forming, morphing, and wiping away historical silhouettes and iconography across key eras.
- **Procedural Soundtrack**:
  - Web Audio API soundtrack generator rendering a continuous musical medley of classic American folk and historic melodies using synthesized acoustic timbres.
- **Autonomous Timing**: Single-click execution running the full 2-minute narrative animation from 1776 to modern times without manual edits.
