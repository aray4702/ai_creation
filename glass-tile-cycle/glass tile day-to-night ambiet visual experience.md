# Glass Tiles from Day to Night

An eight-second, looping ambient film rendered entirely inside one HTML file, with no images, libraries or pre-rendered media.

## Picture
- A grid of translucent, refractive glass tiles that catch the light and cast caustic shadows.
- Koi, cranes and white doves drifting smoothly across the glass.

## Light
- A continuous change from bright daylight to warm golden sunset and on to deep indigo night.

## Shader
- A custom WebGL fragment shader for refraction inside the glass, colour dispersion at the edges, and gentle ripples on the surface.
