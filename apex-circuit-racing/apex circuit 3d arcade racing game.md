# Apex Circuit: 3D Arcade Racer

A full arcade racing game in 3D, with car physics, computer-controlled rivals and a detailed race track.

## Assets
Every asset is made in code: cars, track, scenery (trees, rocks, ground textures) and particle effects. Nothing is downloaded. Blender isn't available, so procedural generation is the only route.

## Driving
- Throttle, brake and steering that feel quick and responsive.
- **Drift → nitro loop:** sliding through tight corners charges a boost meter. Spending it gives a burst of speed with a visible exhaust trail.

## Opponents
- Five AI cars that follow the track on their own, race competitively, and have their speed tuned so races stay close.

## Circuit
- A closed loop with banked corners, climbs and dips, changing light, roadside greenery and clear edge barriers.

## Presentation
- A start countdown before the lights go green.
- On-screen readouts for speed, current lap, race position (for example 2nd of 6) and nitro level.
- Sound: engine note that follows the revs, tyre screech while drifting, a whoosh for nitro, and an energetic music track.
