# Arcade Kart Racer

A complete 3D kart racing game in one HTML file, built with Three.js/WebGL.

## Racers
- A cast of original drivers (names like Blaze, Zippy and Toadley), each with its own kart design and colours.

## Track
- A looping course that rises and falls, with clearly marked edges, speed-boost strips and scenery around it.

## Gameplay
- Snappy handling with drifting, proper bumping and collisions, and speed boosts.
- Mystery item boxes around the track that hand out random power-ups.

## HUD
- Live race standings, lap count, lap time, speedometer and a small overhead map.
