# Neutron Star Particle Simulation

A 3D particle simulation of a rotating neutron star, built with Three.js and WebGL shaders.

## The star
- A dense, intensely bright core wrapped in very strong magnetic field lines and glowing plasma.
- Relativistic particle jets blasting out from both poles of the rotation axis.

## Controls
- Adjust rotation speed, magnetic field strength, jet brightness and how fast time runs.
- A cinematic camera orbits the star so you can see gravitational lensing.
