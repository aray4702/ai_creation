# 15-Second Kinetic Motion Showreel

A high-energy, 15-second motion-graphics showreel in a single HTML file, made with the care of a top motion designer.

## Typography and motion
- Bold kinetic typography, 3D text transformations, smooth ease-in-out transitions and punchy camera cuts.

## Effects
- Procedural particle bursts, chromatic aberration, optical glare, liquid morphing of vector shapes, and fluid canvas animation.
- Colour schemes that shift in time with fast, rhythmic animation cues.

## Performance
- A steady 60 fps using requestAnimationFrame and GPU-accelerated WebGL/Canvas shaders.
