The Antikythera Mechanism

An Interactive 3D Reconstruction

Recovered from a shipwreck off the Greek island of Antikythera (c. 150–100 BCE), this hand-cranked bronze device is the oldest known analog computer — a geared calculator of the heavens.

  • Explode the case with the slider to reveal the internal gear train, including the pin-and-slot lunar-anomaly differential.
  • Click any gear to see its real tooth count and historically documented function.
  • Turn the hand crank (drag it, or use the transport buttons) to drive the Sun pointer, the Moon-phase display, and the Saros eclipse-prediction dial.
