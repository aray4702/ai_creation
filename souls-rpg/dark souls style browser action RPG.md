# Dark Souls–Style Action RPG in the Browser

A third-person action RPG in the spirit of the Dark Souls series, running in a single HTML file on Three.js.

## Setting
- The grounds of a gothic stone castle: stone arches, thick fog, moody changing light and altars holding glowing embers.

## Fighting
- **Player:** a health bar, a stamina bar that attacks and rolls drain, melee attacks, the ability to lock onto a target, and a dodge roll.
- **Enemies:** they walk patrol routes, notice the player, attack in combos, and drop loot when killed.

## Resting at ember altars (the bonfire equivalent)
- Resting at an altar refills health and stamina, resets the area, and brings defeated enemies back.

## Interface
- A dark-fantasy HUD with health and stamina bars, a large boss health bar during boss fights, and item slots.
