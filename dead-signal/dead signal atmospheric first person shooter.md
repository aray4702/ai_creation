# Dead Signal: Atmospheric First-Person Shooter

An atmospheric FPS in which the player defends a remote radio station from waves of hostile robot drones.

## Assets
- Every 3D model (weapon, drones, the radio station, lights and terrain) is generated in code, with no imported assets.

## Shooting
- A first-person view with weapon sway, recoil and magazine reloads.
- A flashlight that casts live beams and hard-edged shadows through the dark.

## Enemies and objective
- Spider-legged and flying drones arrive in waves, move across the terrain, flank, and attack both the station and the player.
- A transmission counter climbs from 0% to 100% as the waves are survived.

## Atmosphere
- An intro loading sequence and a dramatic finale when the transmission completes, with a beam shooting into the sky.
- Warning sirens, eerie sci-fi ambience, mechanical footsteps, gunfire and an alert HUD.
