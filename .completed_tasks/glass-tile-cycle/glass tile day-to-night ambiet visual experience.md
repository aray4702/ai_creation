# Glass Tile Day-to-Night Ambient Visual Experience

Create an ambient 8-second looping visual art film rendered entirely inside one standalone HTML file without external images, libraries, or pre-rendered media.

## Art Direction & Shader Mechanics
- **Visual Composition**:
  - Grid matrix of translucent, refractive glass tiles reflecting light and casting caustic shadows.
  - Animated figures—koi fish, cranes, and white doves—seamlessly drifting across glass surfaces.
- **Lighting Cycle**:
  - Continuous smooth transition from bright daylight hues to warm golden sunset and deep indigo night lighting.
- **Refraction & Material Shader**: Custom WebGL fragment shader calculating internal glass refraction, edge chromatic dispersion, and subtle surface ripples.
