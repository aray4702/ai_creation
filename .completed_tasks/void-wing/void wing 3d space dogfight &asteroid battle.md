# Void Wing: 3D Space Dogfight & Asteroid Battle

Build a full 3D space flight combat simulation game featuring dogfighting against enemy starships and navigation through asteroid fields.

## Requirements & Mechanics
- **Asset Creation Constraint**: Generate the player starship 3D mesh, enemy starships, procedural asteroid meshes with high-resolution surface texturing, lasers, and explosion effects entirely in code/Blender without external media.
- **Flight & Combat Controls**:
  - Pitch, roll, yaw, and throttle propulsion controls for 3D space movement.
  - Weapon firing system: Energy blasters with projectile velocity, raycast collision, and heat/ammo management.
- **AI & Enemy Wave Systems**:
  - Hostile starship AI that maneuvers, tracks player position, and fires plasma bolts.
  - Destructible space debris and high-density asteroid fields that fracture or explode when shot.
- **Visual Effects & HUD**:
  - High-detail ship lighting, engine thruster particle trails, and particle-based explosion impacts.
  - Cockpit/Tactical HUD displaying crosshair reticle, ship shield/hull health, radar/target tracker, and mission status.
- **Sound Design**: Deep-space audio synth score, laser blast audio, and bass-heavy explosion sound effects.

**Execution note:** Blender is not installed: generate every asset procedurally in code.
