Honey Coiling

A real-time viscous-thread simulation: a thin stream of honey falls from a nozzle and buckles into a coiling liquid rope as it lands — the classic "fluid mechanical sewing machine" instability (Ribe; Chiu-Webster & Lister). Adjust pour height, flow, viscosity, surface tension and density and watch the coiling regime change.
