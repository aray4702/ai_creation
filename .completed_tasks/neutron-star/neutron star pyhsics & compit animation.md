Create a 3D astrophysical particle simulation of a rotating Neutron Star using Three.js and WebGL shaders.

### Visual Components:
1. **Star Structure:**
   - Dense hyper-luminous core surrounded by ultra-strong magnetic field lines and plasma glow.
   - Render dual polar relativistic particle jets blasting outward from the rotational axes.
2. **Interactive Controls:**
   - Controls to adjust rotation speed, magnetic field intensity, jet emission brightness, and time acceleration.
   - Cinematic camera orbiting around the star to view gravitational lensing effects.
