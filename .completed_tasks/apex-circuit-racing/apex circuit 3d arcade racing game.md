# Apex Circuit: 3D Arcade Racing Game

Create a complete 3D arcade racing game featuring dynamic car physics, AI opponent drivers, and environmental tracks.

## Requirements & Mechanics
- **Asset Creation Constraint**: Build all 3D vehicle models, racetrack geometry, environmental props (trees, rocks, terrain textures), and particle systems procedurally or via Blender without using external online downloads.
- **Driving Physics & Controls**:
  - Responsive car handling with acceleration, braking, and steering.
  - **Drift & Nitro Mechanics**: Executing drifts around sharp turns builds up a nitro boost meter; activating nitro triggers a visual trail effect and high-speed boost.
- **AI Competitors**:
  - Program 5 AI driver karts/cars that navigate the racetrack autonomously with competitive pathfinding and balanced speed scaling.
- **Track & Environment**:
  - Closed-loop 3D circuit featuring banked turns, height variations, dynamic lighting, atmospheric foliage, and track boundaries.
- **UI & Audio**:
  - Countdown timer prior to race start.
  - Live HUD displaying speedometer, lap counter, track position (e.g., 1st/6th place), and nitro meter.
  - Engine revs, drift tire squeals, nitro blast sound effects, and upbeat background music.

**Execution note:** Blender is not installed: generate every asset procedurally in code.
