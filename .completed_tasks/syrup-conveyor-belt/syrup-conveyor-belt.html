Syrup Conveyor Belt

A thin stream of golden syrup falls onto a moving conveyor belt. At low belt speed the stream coils like a stacked rope; speed it up and the pattern unwinds through meandering and figure-eight ("W") patterns into a straight deposited line — the classic fluid-mechanical-sewing-machine transition (Chiu-Webster & Lister, 2006).
