# Browser-Based Voxel Engine with Shaders & Water Physics

Build a browser-based voxel sandbox game engine ("Minecraft Test") in a single self-contained WebGL HTML application.

## World Generation & Physics
- **Procedural World**: Infinite/chunk-based 3D terrain generated via 3D Simplex/Perlin noise with biomes, trees, caves, and selectable block types.
- **Lighting & Atmosphere**:
  - Dynamic skybox featuring continuous day-and-night cycles with dynamic shadow mapping and sunrise/sunset color transitions.
- **Fluid & Shader Effects**:
  - Real-time liquid water physics with wave motion, opacity reflection, and refraction shaders.
- **Controls**: First-person pointer-lock movement, block destruction/placement physics, and collision detection.
