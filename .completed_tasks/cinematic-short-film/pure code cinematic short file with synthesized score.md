# Pure Code Cinematic Short Film with Synthesized Score

Generate a complete cinematic short film rendered entirely in real-time code without video files, pre-rendered assets, or external video editing tools.

## Production Specifications

- **Real-Time Visual Storytelling**:
  - Scripted 3D or 2D procedural cinematic scenes with camera cuts, depth of field, color grading, and dynamic atmospheric lighting.
  - Character and environmental motion driven entirely by algorithmic animations.
- **Procedural Soundtrack & Audio**:
  - Synthesize a full orchestral/ambient musical score using the Web Audio API.
  - Utilize additive synthesis, physical modeling, and FM synthesis to recreate realistic acoustic instrument sounds (strings, brass, piano, percussion).
- **Format**: Single executable `.html` file that runs autonomous scene progression from start to end title credits.
- **Duration**: 2–3 minutes, 4–6 scenes.
- **Story**: A poor scholar refuses a ghost girl's beauty and her gold; his kindness moves her to save him from the demon that enslaves her, and when he lays her bones to rest, she returns to life, and for the first time the water shows her reflection.
- **Dependencies**: No external libraries (or: CDN-loaded Three.js permitted). No embedded binary/base64 media.
- **Start**: A single "click to begin" screen is permitted (browser autoplay policy); no interaction afterward.
- **Audio bar**: Instruments should be recognizable as their family, not indistinguishable from recordings. Score must sync to scene transitions.
- **Target**: 60fps at 1080p on a recent laptop in Chrome; responsive to window size.
