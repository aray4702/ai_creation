# Dark Souls Style Browser Action RPG

Develop a third-person action RPG inspired by Dark Souls running directly in a single-file HTML/Three.js browser engine.

## Core Gameplay & World Mechanics
- **Environment**: Gothic stone castle grounds with stone archways, atmospheric fog, dynamic lighting, and ember altars.
- **Combat Mechanics**:
  - Player character with stamina management, health bar, melee attacks, lock-on targeting, and dodge rolls.
  - Enemy AI that patrols, detects player presence, initiates melee combos, and drops loot upon defeat.
- **Bonfire / Ember Rest System**:
  - Interactive rest altars that restore player health and stamina, reset world state, and respawn defeated enemies.
- **UI/UX**: Custom dark fantasy HUD featuring health/stamina bars, boss health overlays, and item slots.
