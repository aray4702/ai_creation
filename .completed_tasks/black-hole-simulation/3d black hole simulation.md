# 3D Interactive Black Hole Physics Simulation

Use WebGL and Three.js to generate a high-precision interactive 3D simulation of a black hole.

## Requirements
- **Core Visual Features**:
  - Gravitational lensing effect on background starlight and cosmic dust.
  - Glowing accretion disk with dynamic particle rotation and temperature-based color gradients.
  - Relativistic jets emitting from the poles.
  - Doppler boosting and gravitational redshift visual effects.
- **Interactivity & Camera Controls**:
  - Camera modes: Free Flight, Cinematic Orbit, and Scientific Observation Mode.
  - Interactive parameter controls for Accretion Disk Mass, Spin/Angular Momentum, Tilt Angle, and Light Bending Intensity.
- **Presets**:
  - Sagittarius A*
  - M87*
  - Stellar Mass Black Hole
  - Kerr (Rotating) Black Hole
