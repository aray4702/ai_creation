Gear-Driven Calculator

A working 3D model of a Pascal-style adding machine (1642) with an odometer carry train.

  • Drive gears (20T) under each column turn the accumulator ring gears (20T) 1:1.
  • When a wheel rolls from 9 to 0, its 2-tooth carry lug kicks a 6-tooth transfer pinion, which turns the next wheel exactly one digit (2 teeth of 20).
  • Subtraction turns the drive gears backwards, so borrows ripple the same way.
