Build a functional 3D mechanical calculator model using Three.js based on historical gear-driven calculating engines.

### Specifications:
1. **3D Gear Mechanism Rendering:**
   - Render interconnected brass/metal gears, drive shafts, and numbered dial indicators.
   - Name and label specific mechanical sub-assemblies (e.g., drive gear, accumulator wheel).
2. **Interactive Computation:**
   - Provide an input interface to perform arithmetic operations (e.g., 8 + 9).
   - Upon pressing "Calculate", trigger physical gear rotation sequences that physically drive the indicator dials to spin to the correct output (e.g., 17).
3. **Camera & Inspection:**
   - Support zoom, orbit, and pan camera controls to inspect internal teeth meshing and wheel turn physics from close-up angles.
