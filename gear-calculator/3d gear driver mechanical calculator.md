# Gear-Driven Mechanical Calculator in 3D

Recreate, in Three.js, a working adding machine in the tradition of the historical gear-based calculators.

## The machine
- Show the moving parts: meshing brass/metal gears, shafts, and number dials that display the result.
- Label the key sub-assemblies by name, for example the drive gear and the accumulator wheel.

## Doing a sum
- Give the user a way to enter a calculation such as 8 + 9.
- When they press **Calculate**, the gears visibly turn in sequence and it is that motion that rolls the dials to the correct answer (17 in this example).

## Looking closely
- Orbit, zoom and pan the camera so the user can get close to the teeth and watch how each wheel turns the next.
